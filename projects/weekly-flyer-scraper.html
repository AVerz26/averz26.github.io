Project Purpose & Overview

Supermarket promotional flyers and inserts (encartes) are traditionally published as image carousels on social media platforms like Instagram. Manually reviewing these flyers to track grocery price trends, compare competitor deals, or compile purchasing reports is tedious, error-prone, and time-consuming.

FlyerScout AI converts this manual process into a 100% autonomous pipeline. It crawls target supermarket Instagram accounts, leverages multimodal Vision AI (Google Gemini Flash / GPT-4o) to extract product names and promotional prices directly from images, categorizes items, deduplicates deals with SHA-256 hashes in SQLite, tracks price evolution over time, and pushes daily digests and multi-tab Excel workbooks straight to Telegram.

End-to-End System Pipeline

  • Headless Social Scraping: Automated crawlers monitoring target supermarket Instagram handles via Apify Actor endpoints.
  • Multimodal Vision AI: High-precision text & price optical comprehension via Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (and OpenAI GPT-4o).
  • Categorization & Normalization: Smart rule-based and NLP taxonomy mapping (Carnes, Laticínios, Mercearia, Hortifruti, Limpeza, etc.).
  • SHA-256 Deduplication: Fingerprinting (supermarket + item + price + date) stored in SQLite (data/offers.db).
  • Historical Time-Series Indexing: Structured timestamps allow instant aggregations, moving averages, and competitor spread comparisons.
  • Telegram Bot & Excel Dispatch: Sends structured digests and multi-sheet Excel files (.xlsx) to subscribers every morning.
  • GitHub Actions Cloud CI/CD: Fully automated 07:00 AM serverless execution committing database updates back to repository.
